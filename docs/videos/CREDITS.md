# AgentTM demo video credits

[Watch the 30-second demo](agenttm-demo.mp4) · [Main README](../../README.md)

The video includes background music and transition sounds. It uses real AgentTM
UI captures from a fictional, disposable shopping-cart project, a real managed
Codex recording, and an actual isolated checkpoint restoration. Captures are
framed and animated editorially; this is not an uncut screen recording.

## Background music

**Cat Walk — Arulo**, from [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/house/),
incorporated under the [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/).
A 30-second excerpt is normalized and faded. This is not a grant to redistribute
the standalone music, claim it as original, or register it with Content ID.
